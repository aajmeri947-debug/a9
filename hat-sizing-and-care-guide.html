Measuring & Caring for Hats

A well-fitting hat feels weightless, and a well-kept one lasts for decades. Measure carefully, handle by the brim and store it with its shape supported.

  • 5 chapters
  • 6 min read
  • Reviewed Nov 2026
No. 02
1

Measure your head

Use a soft tape measure around the widest part of your head, about 1 cm above the ears and eyebrows. Keep the tape level and snug but not tight. If you fall between sizes, choose the larger one; a little foam tape inside the sweatband can fine-tune the fit.

2

Check the fit

A good fit is comfortable all day.

  • It should sit level, without rocking or leaving a red line on your forehead.
  • You should be able to slide one finger under the sweatband.
  • It should stay on in a light breeze without being pulled down hard.
3

Handle by the brim

Pick a hat up by the brim at the front and back, not by the pinched crown. Repeatedly pinching the crown weakens felt and can crack straw over time. Put hats down crown first or on their side so the brim keeps its shape.

4

Clean gently

Brush felt hats anticlockwise with a soft brush to lift dust. Wipe straw with a barely damp cloth. Let a damp hat dry naturally on a hat stand or upturned bowl, away from radiators and direct sun, which can shrink felt and make straw brittle.

5

Store with support

Keep hats in a hatbox or on a shelf, upside down on the crown or on a hat stand, stuffed lightly with acid-free tissue. Avoid stacking hats inside each other for long periods, and keep them away from damp, which can cause felt to lose its shape.
